##MLH Fellowship
3 times a year rolling applications for internship altreantive, partners including AWS, Google, Meta, Github, G-Research. 20 hours weekly committment, stipend max 5K USD for duration.

https://fellowship.mlh.com/partners


##Anthropic Fellowship
4 cohorts annually, rolling applications. New cohorts include RL, AI safety, AI security. Working on empirical research questions aligned with Anthropic's research priorities. 

https://alignment.anthropic.com/2025/anthropic-fellows-program-2026/
