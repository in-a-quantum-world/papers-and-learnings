### [Dylan Patel on the AI Chip Race — NVIDIA, Intel & the US Government vs. China](https://a16z.news/p/dylan-patel-on-the-ai-chip-race-nvidia)

- **Saved:** 2026-09-03 · **Tags:** `NVIDIA` `Intel` `Huawei` `semiconductors` `export controls` `SemiAnalysis`
- **My note:** a16z podcast with Dylan Patel (SemiAnalysis), published 2025-09-23. Built around NVIDIA's $5B investment in Intel: Huawei's viability as a domestic Chinese NVIDIA substitute and where its real bottleneck sits (memory production capacity, not logic), NVIDIA's moat and cash deployment, hyperscaler capex, Oracle's positioning via its OpenAI data-centre deals, and infra-level issues like prefill/decode disaggregation and GPU reliability on newer architectures. Good complement to the Etched items above — same compute layer, viewed from the incumbent side.

### [Why compute might get 10x more expensive in coming years](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/why-compute-might-get-10x-more-expensive)

- **Saved:** 2026-09-03 · **Tags:** `compute` `economics` `scaling` `Dwarkesh`
- **My note:** Dwarkesh Patel, 2026-07-29. Argues compute prices rise rather than fall: labs have been 10x-ing revenue annually on only ~3x compute growth, and that gap can't keep coming out of margin. His anchor — a human-level SWE running on an H100 equivalent implies that H100 should rent for >$250k/year, ~15x current spot — with supply pinned by wafer allocation and fab capacity. Implication is a winner-take-most market where new entrants can't buy their way to the frontier.
