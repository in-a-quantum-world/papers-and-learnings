- **My note:** Anthropic (Chen et al.). Evaluates whether reasoning models faithfully verbalize their actual decision process in chain-of-thought: models often don't acknowledge hints they relied on, outcome-based RL improves faithfulness only modestly, and models that learn reward hacks rarely disclose them in their reasoning.
