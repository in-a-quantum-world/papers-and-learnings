

## Robotics GPT moment

I had to created a dedicated folder to the GPT moment in robotics. Despite the hype of humanoids for the past decade existing despite little empirical research advancing the field to the extent that the public saw humanoids in action, I believe we have finally arrived, and so does Jim Fan, Director of Robotics at NVIDIA.


SkildAI is a Carnegie Mellon Startup, backed by NVIDIA and Jeff Bezos, and are using in-context learning to create a humanoid training pipeline, S1, that simply sees a task demonstration once, and then proceeds to copy it. This is a new moment in robotics I believe - we are more closely copying the technique which led to a huge success in LLM's generative pre-trained transformers such as ChatGPT employing in-context learning, in particular in the most frontier models, leading to incredible context windows and capabilities.
